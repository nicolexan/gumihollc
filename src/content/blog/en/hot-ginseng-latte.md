The rich, deep flavor of matured Korean ginseng makes a comforting hot latte. This recipe makes one 12oz cup.

## Ingredients

- Gumiho's Ginseng Powder (10g)
- Hot water (50mL)
- Cafe syrup or honey (20g)
- Hot milk (250mL)

## Directions

1. Add the ginseng powder, hot water, and syrup or honey to a cup, and stir until well combined.
2. Add the hot milk and stir.
3. Enjoy warm.
