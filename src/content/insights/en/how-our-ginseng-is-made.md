Our ginseng products are sourced and manufactured in Geumsan, where centuries-old traditions and advanced techniques converge to produce ginseng of unmatched quality.

## Sourced from Geumsan

Geumsan is famous across Korea for its top-quality ginseng. We work with the region's most fertile and trusted farms, using genuine, matured Korean ginseng.

## As Pure as Possible

We take great care in making our ginseng powder as pure as possible. There are no added chemicals, sugar, or artificial ingredients: just wholesome, natural goodness. Advanced techniques preserve the rich flavor of the ginseng, while yam enhances the taste and texture, making every drink a treat to enjoy.

## Imported Directly

We import our products directly into the U.S., ensuring they retain their natural potency and flavor from Geumsan to your table.
