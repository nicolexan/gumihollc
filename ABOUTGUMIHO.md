*Introduction to Korean Ginseng*
Korean ginseng (Panax ginseng), also known as "Insam" in Korea, is a revered root that has been part of Korean culture and tradition for centuries. Known for its versatility, Korean ginseng is widely used as a dietary supplement and wellness ingredient. Its primary active components, ginsenosides, are believed to contribute to its reputation as a natural resource for supporting general health and vitality. 

This root is commonly consumed in various forms, such as tea, powder, extract, or dried slices. Its use is steeped in cultural significance, often symbolizing vitality and endurance.
